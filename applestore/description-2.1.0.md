# App Store description, 2.1.0 draft

Tomo TV turns your Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad into the front end for your Jellyfin media server. Pick a video, it plays. No setup gymnastics, no codec headaches. Free and open source, no subscription.

NEVER LOSE YOUR PLACE

- Top Shelf: a live Continue Watching row right on your Apple TV home screen
- Continue Watching picks up where you left off and lines up the next episode
- Up Next: the next episode appears over the credits with a countdown, and the player's info panel lists your queue so you can jump anywhere
- Skip Intro and Skip Credits buttons on Apple TV, when your server publishes markers

IN THE PLAYER

- Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus reach your receiver untouched
- Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, PCM and FLAC play losslessly, with 6.1 and 7.1 layouts intact and 24-bit sources still 24-bit
- Multi-audio track switching mid-playback for dual-language and commentary tracks
- Subtitles: embedded and external (.srt) text tracks in the native picker, plus picture-based subtitles (PGS, DVD/VobSub) decoded right on the device
- Picture in Picture on iPhone and iPad, starting automatically when you leave the app mid-video
- Quality presets: Original (untouched, the default), 480p, 540p, 720p, 1080p, and 4K
- HDR10 and HLG playback
- AirPlay support, with poster and title on the Lock Screen

YOUR WHOLE LIBRARY

- Browse it all: movies, shows, seasons, episodes, collections, music, playlists, and photos
- Native search by title, genre, artist, or year
- Native queue player for music and audiobooks: gapless transitions, background playback on iPhone, and Lock Screen controls
- Photo viewer and slideshow
- Filter by favorites, genres, years, artists, and played status, with a shuffle mode
- Long-press any card to mark it watched or favorite
- Ambient artwork backgrounds while you browse
- Demo Mode to try the app instantly, no server required thanks to the official Jellyfin demo server

CONNECT IN SECONDS

- Quick Connect: approve from any Jellyfin app, no typing passwords on the remote
- Username and password login
- Type just an IP and Tomo TV finds the right protocol and port automatically
- Or scan your local network and pick your server from a list, nothing to type
- Save multiple servers and switch between them
- If your server's address changes, Tomo TV finds it again on its own, no re-login
- Works on your home network or over the internet

SETUP

1. Run Jellyfin on your Mac, PC, or NAS
2. Enter your server URL, or just type its IP address
3. Start watching

THE ENGINE

Built natively with AVPlayer, Tomo TV plays H.264 and HEVC in any container (MKV, AVI, WMV, and more) right on your device, no server transcoding needed. Your soundtrack is left alone: Atmos and Dolby Digital pass straight through, and TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio and FLAC stay lossless. Even picture-based subtitles are decoded on the device, so a disc rip keeps its original video and audio instead of being re-encoded. Legacy video formats like VP9, MPEG-2, and MPEG-4 convert locally on your device's own media silicon. Your server only steps in for the rare leftovers, like 4K in exotic codecs and interlaced video, so everything just plays.

REQUIREMENTS

- A Jellyfin server (10.8 or later recommended)
- Skip Intro and Skip Credits appear when your server provides intro and credits markers

PRIVACY

No analytics. No tracking. No ads. Your credentials stay in the device Keychain. Video streams directly from your server to your device.
